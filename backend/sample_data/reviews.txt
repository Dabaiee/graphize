Review of the AeroPress Coffee Maker: I've owned this for three years and it's
the most durable brewer I own. Aerobie clearly built it to last. It makes a clean,
smooth cup and travels well. Compared to the Hario V60, the AeroPress is far more
forgiving for beginners.

Review of the Fellow Stagg Kettle: Gorgeous gooseneck kettle from Fellow. The
temperature control is precise to the degree, which matters for the Chemex
Pour-Over. Pricey, but pairs perfectly with the Fellow Ode Grinder for a complete
setup.

Review of the Baratza Encore Grinder: The Encore is the classic entry-level
grinder. Baratza's customer support is legendary — they sent me replacement burrs
for free. Grind consistency is great for the AeroPress and the Hario V60. If you
can afford it, the Fellow Ode Grinder is a step up.

Review of the Chemex Pour-Over 6-Cup: A beautiful brewer. Chemex has been making
these since 1941 and it shows. You must use the Chemex Filters — thinner filters
ruin the clarity. Best paired with the Fellow Stagg Kettle for controlled pouring.

Review of the Hario V60 Dripper: Hario's V60 is a Japanese classic. It's cheap and
makes an excellent cup, but it's less forgiving than the AeroPress. The Timemore
Scale helps a lot with nailing the ratio.
